Controlled

One remote for the devices at home, on your phone. Today: your LG TV over Wi-Fi (d-pad, volume, your apps, typing, turning it on) and your Windows PC over Bluetooth, as a keyboard and trackpad with nothing to install on the PC.

Join the test

Android. No ads, no account.

Free, forever

Controlled runs entirely on your phone and costs us nothing to run, so we will never charge for it. If we ever add something that does cost money to run, like an AI feature on a server, only that part could be paid: at cost, plus a small fee we show up front. Everything Controlled does today stays free.

How to install

Controlled is in a test on Google Play before it goes public.

  1. Tap Join the test and join the Google group, with the same Google account as your Play Store.
  2. Open the test on Google Play and tap Become a tester. If Play can't find it yet, try again in a day or two: the test opens once Google approves it.
  3. Install it from Google Play. For the TV, open Controlled on the same Wi-Fi as your TV and choose Accept on the TV. For a PC, choose Add a PC and follow the steps.
