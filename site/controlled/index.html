Controlled

A remote for your LG TV on your phone: the d-pad, volume, your apps, a keyboard and a touchpad. It talks only to your TV, over your home Wi-Fi.

Get it on Google Play

Free. No ads, no account. Android.

How to install

Controlled is in a private test on Google Play, so the button works only once your Google email is on the testers list. Write to jcrubioa2@gmail.com with your Google email.

  1. Tap Get it on Google Play, signed in with the same Google account as your Play Store, and tap Accept invite.
  2. Install it from Google Play.
  3. Open Controlled with your phone on the same Wi-Fi as your TV, pick your TV and choose Accept on the TV.
